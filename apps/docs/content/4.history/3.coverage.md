---
title: Coverage and refreshability
description: Keep stored coverage refreshability separate from Provider result quality.
---

Coverage records which absolute half-open intervals have completed. Empty coverage means a
completed capture found no matching data. Missing intervals are captured; provisional intervals are
refreshed until their Provider marks them stable.

When a Provider returns `meta.freshness.provisionalFrom`, History stores a crossing range as a stable
prefix and a provisional suffix. Later synchronization fetches only that suffix. Use `expire()` to
delete selected older ranges without changing the public range model.

Provider Quality remains separate: approximation, sampling, thresholding, warnings, and partial
results describe the source response rather than whether stored coverage will change.
