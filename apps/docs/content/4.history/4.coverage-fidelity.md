---
title: Coverage and fidelity
description: Distinguish retained ranges and transformations from Provider result quality.
---

History Fidelity is range-scoped. `full` means the retained representation was preserved,
`reduced` lists deliberate transformations, and `not-preserved` identifies missing or provisional
intervals. Empty full-fidelity coverage means a completed capture found no matching data.

Coverage records which absolute half-open intervals have completed. `compact()` replaces duplicate
retained segments and `expire()` deletes selected older ranges without changing this public range
model.

Provider Quality remains separate: approximation, sampling, thresholding, freshness, warnings,
and partial results describe the source response rather than storage preservation.
