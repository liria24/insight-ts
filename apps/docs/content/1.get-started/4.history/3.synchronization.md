---
title: Synchronization
description: Fetch missing or provisional ranges and commit each partition incrementally.
---

`history.sync({ range })` reads coverage, fetches only missing or provisional intervals, and writes
each planned partition idempotently. A partition becomes complete only after all of its Provider
pages have completed and its replacement is durable.

Queries use the same synchronization workflow for uncovered ranges before materializing the
requested result.
