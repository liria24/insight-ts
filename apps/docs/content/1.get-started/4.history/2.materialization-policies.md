---
title: Materialization and policies
description: Preserve canonical data in bounded partitions with explicit reductions.
---

Canonical adapters may provide an internal History materializer. Metrics preserve aggregation and
ratio inputs; Logs and Traces preserve stable IDs and order. The Engine partitions large event
ranges, drains native pagination inside one partition, and commits it before fetching the next.

Materializers describe capture and reconstruction only. The Engine owns gap planning, stable
segment identity, reductions, coverage, and Fidelity, so repositories remain capability-agnostic.

Policies may match a Scope, capability, and optional range, then sample, filter, truncate, or run
an explicitly named custom reduction. Reduced segments retain the transformation list in Fidelity
metadata. Repositories may compress losslessly, but never apply hidden reductions.

```ts
createHistory({
    repository,
    policies: [{ capability: 'logs', transformations: [{ kind: 'sample', rate: 0.1 }] }],
})
```
